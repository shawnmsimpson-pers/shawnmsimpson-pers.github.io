SwingCoach

A private, personal tool used by one person — its author — to record and review baseball and softball swings in a home batting cage, for coaching his own children.

It is not a product, it is not distributed, and it has no users other than its author.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Who this covers

SwingCoach is operated by a single individual for personal family use. There are no accounts, no sign-ups and no other users. The only Google account it ever touches is the author’s own.

What Google data it accesses

SwingCoach requests one Google scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file.

That scope grants access only to files the application itself creates in the author’s own Google Drive. It cannot read, modify, move or delete any other file in that Drive, including the folders it writes into, and it cannot see anything the author stores there independently of this application.

SwingCoach requests no other Google scope, and accesses no other Google service.

What it stores there, and why

SwingCoach keeps an offsite backup copy of its own analysis corpus — swing video clips recorded in the author’s own batting cage, together with the measurement files derived from them. The purpose is solely to survive the loss of the local machine or its attached storage.

The recordings are of the author and his own children, made by him in his own home. They are stored in his own Google Drive, under his own account, counting against his own storage.

Who the data is shared with

No one. The data is not shared, published, sold, transmitted to any third party, or used for advertising, profiling, training, or any purpose other than the author’s own coaching review. There is no analytics, no telemetry and no external processing service.

Retention and deletion

The author controls the data entirely and can delete any of it at any time by deleting the files or folders from his own Google Drive, or by revoking the application’s access from his Google Account security settings. Revoking access immediately ends the application’s ability to reach any file.

Security

The application’s credentials are stored with owner-only permissions on a single machine on the author’s home network. The application runs only on that machine and is not reachable from the public internet.

Changes

If the application’s use of Google data changes, this page will be updated before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the author at the support address listed on the application’s Google consent screen.

This page exists so the application’s Google OAuth consent screen can link to a description and a privacy policy, as Google requires.
